# Risks and terms

Kano Proxy is an unofficial, independent project. Using a subscription account through it may break that provider's terms, and providers have restricted accounts used this way. Read this page before you connect one.

The app shows the same points as a short usage notice the first time you sign in, and again when the notice changes. You tick a box and choose **Agree and continue** before you can use the app.

## What happens when you connect a subscription account

To use a subscription account, Kano Proxy signs in and sends requests the way that provider's own command-line tool does. The requests come from the server Kano Proxy runs on, not from the provider's app.

This applies to Claude Code, Codex, Grok and Antigravity accounts. Custom endpoints and local models are different. You bring your own API key or your own model server, and requests go where you point them.

## The points in the notice

- **Not affiliated.** Kano Proxy is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI. Their names and marks belong to them. They appear here only to say which accounts Kano Proxy works with.
- **It signs in like the official tools.** Doing this outside the provider's own app may break that provider's terms.
- **Accounts can be restricted.** Providers have limited, suspended and permanently banned accounts used this way, without warning. What a provider does to your account is between you and that provider. Kano Proxy cannot undo it.
- **Use your own accounts.** Connect only accounts you hold and judge you may use this way. Most providers forbid sharing an account or reselling access. If you share an account through Kano Proxy, that is your responsibility.
- **Endpoints can stop working.** The provider endpoints Kano Proxy uses are not publicly documented. A provider can change them at any time, and an account can stop working until Kano Proxy is updated.
- **Provided as is.** The software is released under the MIT License and comes without warranty. You are responsible for the accounts and keys you connect and how you use them.

Each provider's own terms decide what you may do with your account. Read them before you connect it. They change, so read the current version.

## A hosted instance

Someone else may run Kano Proxy for you. Its operator may have its own terms of service and privacy notice. The sign-in page links them, and the usage notice asks you to agree to them as well.
